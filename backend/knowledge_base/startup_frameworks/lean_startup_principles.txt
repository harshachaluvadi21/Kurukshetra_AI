Title: The Lean Startup Framework - Minimum Viable Product (MVP) and Market Sizing
Author: Startup Vault
Category: Startup Frameworks

The core principle of evaluating any startup idea is validating the market need with the smallest possible investment. This is known as the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) approach. 
When sizing a market (TAM, SAM, SOM), investors look for a Total Addressable Market of at least $1 Billion to justify venture-scale returns. A strong CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) of >10% indicates a rapidly expanding market where a startup can capture value even against incumbents.

Macro trends such as AI automation, remote work infrastructure, and aging population demographics are the primary drivers for new market creation in the current decade.
