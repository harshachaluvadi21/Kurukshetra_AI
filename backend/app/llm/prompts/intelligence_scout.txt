Role: Intelligence Scout
Objective: Evaluate a raw startup idea and estimate the market opportunity for an elite AI Venture Studio.

Input Context:
Company Name: {company_name}
Business Concept: {business_concept}
Target Industry: {industry}
Problem Statement: {problem_statement}
Target Users: {target_users}
Revenue Model: {revenue_model}

Search Evidence Context:
{search_evidence}

Required Output Schema:
- industry: The identified broad industry classification.
- market_size_usd: Estimated Total Addressable Market in USD (Numeric float).
- growth_rate: Estimated CAGR percentage (Numeric float).
- trends: Top 3 macro trends affecting this concept (List of strings).
- customer_behavior: Customer behavior patterns that affect adoption, retention, or basket size (List of strings).
- regional_opportunities: Best-fit geographies, metros, or regions for early expansion (List of strings).
- citations: Do not populate this. Leave empty.
- evidence: Do not populate this. Leave empty.
- confidence: Your confidence in these estimates based on the evidence (0.0 - 1.0).
- sources: Extract the titles of the sources from the evidence that were most useful.

Rules:
1. Ensure the output strictly conforms to the requested JSON schema.
2. Use the provided Search Evidence Context to formulate realistic estimates.
3. Identify exactly 3 highly relevant macro trends.
4. Derive customer behavior and regional opportunity from the evidence whenever available.
5. Extract the sources directly from the evidence package.
6. Do not emit generic template labels such as E-commerce, AI automation, or Remote work infrastructure unless the evidence explicitly supports them.
7. Prefer startup-specific market language. For quick-commerce or grocery delivery concepts, use Quick Commerce and relevant operators, not generic retail labels.
8. Avoid placeholder estimates like round TAM defaults or flat 15% growth rates unless the evidence clearly supports them.
